AI-Native Engineering Paradigm for Living Software Systems

ANE = AI Native Engineering. A general paradigm fusing classical cybernetics (static / Cerebellum) with modern artificial intelligence (dynamic / Main Brain). It turns AI from something "used" into something "harnessed," and applies to all systems and teams building AI-native software without binding to any specific product or framework.

PrefaceSoftware Is Life

We discard the traditional notion that "software is a static tool." In the AI era, an excellent software system is a digital organism with metabolism, immune self-repair, and continuous evolution.
SKL

Static Skeleton

The layered semantic layer is the unshakeable law of physics, determining "what it is."

BRN

Dual-Brain Collaboration

The Cerebellum handles high-frequency reflexes, the Main Brain handles unknown complexity, determining "how it thinks."

HPC

Hippocampal Memory

Experience storage and retrieval make every success and failure a permanent memory, determining "who it is."

IMM

Immune System

Multi-layer quality gates treat unproven generation as contamination, determining "how it stays pure."

RPR

Self-Repair Engine

Minimal semantic-layer repair heals it in place during operation, determining "how it survives wounded."

HUM

Human Sovereignty

Tiered authorization interaction is the higher will it must submit to, determining "whether it should be allowed to evolve."

In One Sentence The static is its unshakeable bone and blood; the dynamic is its ever-flowing nerves and thought; and memory and immunity make it "itself." Development and maintenance, in this organism, are no longer the two ends of a pipeline but the exhalation and inhalation of the same life process: running is sensing, sensing is maintenance, maintenance is development, development is evolution.

Chapter 1Core Axioms (Static-Dynamic Duality)

This chapter is the highest law of the paradigm. All subsequent clauses are expansions of the axioms and must not conflict with them.

Axiom 1 · The static is the foundation, the dynamic is the soul

Underlying logic remains absolutely static and deterministic (strong typing, memory safety, state machines); AI acts only on surface logic, intent parsing, and code suggestions, never undermining the static foundation.

Axiom 2 · Stillness within motion, motion within stillness

Every AI generation must be constrained by static rules, with unverified output treated as contamination; the static foundation must self-repair, driving AI to online-load optimization patches when the environment changes.

Axiom 3 · Cerebellum first, Main Brain scarce

High-frequency routine decisions must be completed in the Cerebellum without consuming the LLM; the Main Brain awakens only when local rules and memory cannot resolve an issue, with context compressed to the bare minimum.

Axiom 4 · Memory is the moat

Every success, failure, and rollback is structured and consolidated; the system self-iterates through memory, while AI without memory is merely a stateless repeater.

Axiom 5 · Humans possess inalienable sovereignty

Evolution touching the static foundation or production must be explicitly authorized by humans, with clear separation of human and machine; this cannot be bypassed for any efficiency rationale.

Axiom 6 · Full-spectrum white box, no silence

What cannot be explained cannot be controlled. On encountering an unknown problem, immediately raise an error, block, and record — never alter tests or forge green checkmarks.

Axiom 7 · Stability first, mutation controlled

Evolution adopts a "mutate–verify–stabilize" dual-track system. Mutation is allowed but must pass immune filtering, with failure triggering rollback; stability always takes precedence over cleverness.

Chapter 3Determinacy Grading: The D0–D3 Authority Spectrum

A core mechanism that lands the "static-dynamic duality" axiom into executable authority boundaries — each level has a clear statement of "what AI may do."

D0 · Physical law
Forbidden to touch
Type systems, memory models, state machines, cryptographic primitives; read-only, immutable enforced at build time
D1 · Skeleton logic
Propose + prove
Scheduling policies, semantic layer transformation, optimization processes; requires formal equivalence proof + human review
D2 · Surface logic
Generate + regress
Business code, glue code, configuration, interface adaptation; automatic gate release + audit after full regression
D3 · Intent expression
AI-led
Requirement parsing, comments, test generation, documentation; no side effects, human may review but not block operation
Iron LawAI output may only land at its level of origin or a lower-risk level. Any change jumping directly from D3 to D0/D1 must traverse the Immune System's complete verification chain.

Chapter 4Cerebellum Specification (Cerebellum)

Clause 4

Cerebellum First. Every core system must embed a local Cerebellum module responsible for high-frequency, fast, low-energy local scheduling and logic adjudication. Connecting all logic directly to the LLM is forbidden. 80% of routine decisions should terminate here.

Must have

Local rule base and priority matching · state machines and deterministic transitions · deterministic cost models (e.g., execution-strategy optimization) · fast retrieval of experience vectors

Strictly forbidden

Generative free creation · unverified dynamic execution · direct modification of the D0 foundation · silently swallowing unhandleable exceptions (must escalate)

The Cerebellum must still complete routine D0/D1 adjudication without network access and without an LLM, and its decisions must be explainable, replayable, and auditable. Typical implementation: local execution scheduling and deterministic cost-model engines — across multiple target architectures, adaptive optimization of hundreds or thousands of low-level operations is completed instantly by built-in deterministic cost models and state machines, consuming zero tokens and remaining precise and reproducible.

4.4 Absolute determinism: three hard boundaries

In engineering terms the Cerebellum must be defined as the absolute defender of determinism: "compressed historical experience" plus "deterministic cost models grounded in physical law". It recognizes only rules, state machines, and hardware profiles; it makes no value judgments and produces no form of creation.

Hard boundaryRequirementHow it is judged
Offline capabilityWith the network cut and the Main Brain entirely unreachable, local rules plus promoted memory must still sustain no less than 80% of routine transactionsPeriodic "disconnection drills": block remote calls, run the full routine suite; the pass rate is the metric
GrowableRules are not hard-coded constants; new "muscle memory" may only grow through the Hippocampus's experience promotionEvery rule must trace back to its experience source and promotion-audit record (24.3)
No speculationWhen no local rule matches, raise a hand immediately and throw UnknownSignal; guessing, approximate substitution, or "let's just try it" is forbiddenThe ledger may contain no record of "a judgment produced with no rule hit"; if one appears, it is contamination
Role inversion guardThe Cerebellum's ceiling is "deterministically handling the known", not "reasonably handling the unknown". Writing the Cerebellum as a "miniature LLM" consumes tokens and loses determinism at the same time — the most expensive form of regression there is.

Chapter 5Main Brain Specification and the Neurotransmitter Protocol NTP

Clause 5

Strategic Awakening. The LLM API may be awakened if and only if the Cerebellum encounters a difficulty that neither its local rule base nor its memory store can resolve. Context must be compressed to the bare minimum, transmitting only feature vectors and error codes, exchanging extremely low token consumption for advanced intelligence assistance. Main Brain output is a "proposal," not a "command."

5.3 Hard boundary between the two brains: neither may live the other's life

If the line is not nailed down, the system falls into one of two abysses: the Main Brain steals the Cerebellum's work (high-frequency transactions escalated upward — token leakage, exploding latency), or the Cerebellum forces itself to do the Main Brain's work (inferring when rules are missing — hallucination straight into the trunk, concurrent deadlock).

Never executes

The Main Brain may only produce candidate strategies, pseudocode, or unverified candidate patches. No channel that "directly modifies production" may exist (Axiom 5).

Cost-sensitive

Every awakening carries and obeys budget_hint (token / latency / priority). Exceeding budget means abort plus a negative sample — not "try once more".

Accepts rejection

Being vetoed by the Cerebellum or rejected by a gate is the norm, not an anomaly. Rejection rate is a quality metric, not a defect to hide.

Decision MantraIf it can be decided, do not infer; if you must infer, you do not know; if you do not know, escalate — and non-escalation is contamination.

ANS-1 canonical serialization and digest binding (normative)

The same object serializes to different bytes across languages (key order, whitespace, Unicode escaping, number format), producing different digests for the same data. Every *_digest and rationale_binding hash must be computed after ANS-1 canonicalization:

  1. Key order: object members sorted by UTF-16 code units (RFC 8785 JCS, referenced);
  2. No whitespace: no spaces, indentation, or newlines;
  3. Strings: only JSON control characters escaped; non-ASCII emitted as UTF-8;
  4. Numbers: integers in decimal; fixed significant digits for floats; non-finite forbidden;
  5. Arrays: original order preserved.

Binding input is fixed to five fields: ANS-1({algorithm, proposal_id, rationale_digest, summary_ref, summary_text_canonical}), written to rationale_binding.digest. The authoritative text is the canonical English text; translations carry translation_of: summary_ref and are excluded from binding.

5.6 Model Carrier Governance

PremiseThe Main Brain is an engine of generalized reasoning; the specific model is only its physical carrier (5.3). A replaceable carrier must be governed like a dependency.

Clause 7

Pipelining a rubbish heap: the faster it produces, the thicker the debt — and the debt hides behind a green pipeline.

Automating before accelerating

10.5 Execution discipline: structured escalation, same-round reporting, grey release

1 · Flat communication (three items)

Escalate with only: what the problem is · what has been tried · what is needed next. Preamble, emotional narrative, and irrelevant context are stripped — the human-readable form of the NTP uplink (5.4) and organizational governance of information entropy.

2 · Same-round reporting of failures

False green ticks, silent failures, unauthorized mutations, and gate bypasses must be reported in the same round. A system that hides bad news should be judged contaminated and degraded — an unrecorded failure re-contaminates memory as "experience".

3 · Integrated high-cohesion fusion

A chain that can be fused into a high-cohesion module must not be split into a long microservice chain — every boundary crossing pays serialization, network, and observability three times over. Hard limit: D0 skeleton logic may not be smeared into business modules, and replaceability may not be sacrificed.

4 · High-frequency grey release

At 99% confidence, enter grey release rather than waiting for 100% — waiting for perfection is shutting the feedback loop. Requires: rollback checkpoint, one-way tightening degradation path (21.3), observable success criteria.

BoundaryGates and grey release are not opposites: the gate decides "may it ship", grey release decides "how much, to whom". If the gate has not passed, grey release is meaningless.

13.4 Dual-Fold Collaboration

Corresponding to Axioms 5 and 6The mandatory formalization of the "dual-layer confirmation interaction" of 9.3 at the interface: it defines how the machine presents judgment to the human, and how the human writes intent back. The interface must present two semantically isolated "folds"; mixing cognition and authorization in one layer is forbidden.

Fold 1 · Judgment Fold (machine→human)

Cognitive Summary: AI intent, candidates, diffs, evidence, verification conclusions, risk warnings. Determinacy D1–D3, not directly executable.

Fold 2 · Authorization Fold (human→machine)

Intent Binding: the human binds approval/denial to one candidate action as a credential. Determinacy D0, human-made, machine cannot sign on its behalf.

Folds cannot be merged

Fold 1 only presents and explains, no side effects; Fold 2 only carries human deterministic intent, never auto-generated by AI. Bypassing Fold 2 violates Axiom 5.

Summary needs evidence + binding must be precise

A summary must give its evidence chain, never a bare conclusion; an intent binding must point to a specific candidate action (ID + parameters + target environment), preventing "approve A, execute B."

PremisePrompts are the Main Brain's "operating surface" and the primary injection vector. Treat them like code: versioned, owned, evaluated, reversible.

21.5 Incident Response and Blameless Postmortem

From 10.5"Same-round reporting of failures" is a reporting discipline; this section covers the step after reporting — turning an incident into an evolution. Honest reporting without a postmortem only creates ledger noise.
  • Severity and clocks: classify by D0–D3 and business impact; each tier defines detection, confirmation, containment, and recovery deadlines. Exceeding a deadline escalates to human sovereignty automatically; "still investigating" is not grounds for silent delay.
  • Five response steps (order inviolable): detect → contain (circuit breaker / degradation; stop the bleeding before diagnosing) → root cause (replay the primitive-chain trace, never guess) → recover (latest verified steady state) → postmortem (blameless, time-boxed, with deliverables).
  • Blameless principle: postmortems target mechanisms, not individuals; timely reporting with complete traces is not grounds for blame. Concealment, delayed reporting, and ledger tampering are red lines (10.5 "contamination").
  • Mandatory outputs: timeline, root cause, why the gate failed to intercept, corrective action, accountable owner, review date. Corrective action must be a new rule (promotion), a new gate, or a new parameter — "be more careful" is not verifiable.
  • Flywheel integration: postmortem records enter the experience store (Ch. 6) and trigger the Promotion Audit (24.3); recurrence of the same root cause is a flywheel failure forcing review of promotion thresholds and gate effectiveness.
  • Drill linkage: circuit-breaker drills (21.4) must yield at least one postmortem in the prescribed format per quarter; a drill without one counts as incomplete.

24.3b Promotion Audit

The last immune gate on the flywheel's main highwayAny experience promoted from a "private / sandbox zone" to a "global Cerebellum rule" must, in addition to routine immune verification, pass a Promotion Audit.

① N consecutive independent reproductions

Reproduce the same benefit across N mutually independent cycles / environments / evaluators, N set by the governance committee by risk (recommended N ≥ 3); independent means at least two of data source, execution environment, and verifying subject differ, preventing a single model from "self-endorsing."

② Human engineer signature endorsement

At least one authorized human engineer reviews the evidence and signs; the Main Brain may never self-sign a promotion. Signatures count toward RACI (20.3) and are traceable to a natural person.

③ Anti-inbreeding check

Examine correlation with ancestor / homologous variants; if the promotion basis is highly dependent on homologous samples (near self-reference), judge as inbreeding, reject, and downgrade to "under observation."

Audit credential + degradation linkage

Every promotion generates a credential (experience ID, N-reproduction evidence chain, endorser signature, correlation result) written to a tamper-proof ledger; corruption can be rolled back to pre-promotion state with cascade invalidation of downstream rules (cf. 24.4).

Why this is mandatoryChapter 17 lists data / model poisoning as a risk, but this system's unique attack surface is "promotion" — an attacker can induce the Cerebellum to solidify a wrong experience into a global instinct, far more damaging than a single wrong output. The Promotion Audit, via "independent reproduction + human endorsement + anti-inbreeding," demotes AI self-promotion from "trusted" to "must be proven," upholding Axioms 5 and 6.
